Uber, Wayve and Waymo head toward a London robotaxi showdown

Uber customers in the UK can join an interest list to increase their chances of being matched with a Wayve robotaxi. The move puts London in line for a visible autonomous-vehicle test as Uber, Wayve, and Waymo compete for early rider mindshare.

Uber tells London to get ready for robotaxis

Uber opened a London interest list for riders who want to try Wayve driverless cars when service begins later this year. The rollout is an early test of driverless ridehailing appetite outside the US and China.

WWDC 2026: how to watch and what to expect

Apple’s developer keynote is expected to spotlight operating-system updates and may include a long-awaited Siri overhaul. Apple Intelligence remains under scrutiny after a long period of investor and developer impatience.

Apple investors look for AI overhaul to power next leg of gains
