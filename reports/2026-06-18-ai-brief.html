Industry AI Today

Pixi's new iOS app turns text messages into interactive AR experiences

Pixi is positioning interactive augmented reality as the next layer of mobile messaging, moving beyond stickers, GIFs, and emoji reactions. The launch sits at the overlap of consumer AI, social apps, and AR interfaces.

TechCrunch

Waymo recalls nearly 4,000 robotaxis over construction-zone behavior

Waymo is recalling almost 4,000 robotaxis after identifying cases where vehicles drove into highway sections closed for construction. The item is a reminder that autonomy progress is still being tested hardest in edge-case road conditions.

TechCrunch

How to turn off AI in Google Docs

Google Docs users looking to avoid Gemini writing prompts now have clearer steps for disabling the feature. The story captures the growing tension between AI-by-default product strategy and user control.

TechCrunch

Enterprise AI ROI remains unsettled

NEA's Tiffany Luck framed enterprise AI adoption as a period of experimentation after heavy usage and fast-rising costs. The near-term pressure is shifting from access to measurable return.

TechCrunch

World leaders want American AI without a remote off switch

At the G7, leaders including France's Emmanuel Macron and India's Narendra Modi reportedly raised concerns that access to U.S. AI systems could be cut off overnight. The discussion follows disruption around Anthropic access and points to AI sovereignty as a policy fault line.

TechCrunch

Anthropic joins Frontier carbon removal coalition

Anthropic became the first AI startup to join Frontier, the carbon removal coalition that received another $915 million in pledges. The move links AI infrastructure growth with pressure to account for energy and climate impact.

TechCrunch

Midjourney moves from image generation to full-body ultrasound scans

Midjourney CEO David Holz showed a hardware project called The Midjourney Scanner, an ultrasound-based full-body scanner the company says is aimed at MRI-like image quality. The move is a striking expansion from generative imagery into medical-adjacent hardware.

The Verge

Anthropic hit by export rules few people understand

Anthropic has been fighting to restore access to its newest models after a U.S. government order forced restrictions affecting foreign nationals, including some users inside the U.S. The dispute illustrates how AI export controls can collide with product operations.

The Verge

Two-thirds of Americans think AI is advancing too quickly

A Pew Research poll cited by The Verge found chatbot usage rising sharply, while 63 percent of Americans say AI is advancing too quickly. The result suggests adoption and public comfort are not moving in lockstep.

The Verge

Google Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer to join OpenAI

Noam Shazeer, who spent two decades at Google and co-founded Character.AI, is set to join OpenAI after returning to Google in 2024. The move underscores the continuing competition for senior model-building talent.
