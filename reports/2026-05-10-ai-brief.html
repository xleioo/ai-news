行业 AI 今日新闻

01 · Reuters

EU countries, lawmakers clinch provisional deal on watered-down AI rules

EU governments and lawmakers reached a provisional deal to soften landmark AI rules, including delayed implementation. The move keeps Europe’s AI rulebook moving, while critics warn it may dilute earlier safeguards.

02 · Reuters

US, China weigh launch of official discussions on AI, WSJ reports

Washington and Beijing are considering formal talks on AI, according to a Reuters report citing the Wall Street Journal. The item points to AI safety and strategic competition becoming a direct diplomatic channel.

03 · Reuters

Musk v Altman and the 'manifest destiny' of AI

Reuters’ Artificial Intelligencer column frames the Musk–Altman fight as a wider struggle over who gets to define the future of AI institutions. The piece is useful context for governance debates around OpenAI and the broader sector.

04 · The Verge

Pentagon strikes classified AI deals with OpenAI, Google, and Nvidia — but not Anthropic

The Verge reports a set of classified-setting AI deals involving major AI infrastructure and model providers, while Anthropic is absent from the reported group. The story underlines how national-security adoption is becoming a central AI market.

05 · The Verge

Meta employees are reportedly “miserable” between looming layoffs and AI push

The Verge summarizes reporting that Meta’s aggressive AI internal rollout, employee activity tracking, and planned staff cuts have created anxiety inside the company. The operational question is whether AI-driven productivity pushes are improving work or merely increasing pressure.

06 · The Verge

Gmail’s “Help me write” AI tool will draft emails that sound more like you

Google is rolling out personalization for Gmail’s AI writing feature so drafts can better match a user’s tone and pull context from Workspace. The update moves office AI from generic drafting toward identity- and context-aware assistance.

07 · The Verge

OpenAI launched a Codex extension for Chrome

The Verge notes a Chrome extension that lets Codex work inside signed-in websites and apps through task-specific tab groups. It is another step toward browser-native agent workflows for everyday knowledge work.

08 · The Verge

Cloudflare is laying off 1,100 workers as its AI usage increases by 600 percent

Cloudflare framed the layoffs as part of adapting to an agentic-AI operating model rather than a standard cost-cutting exercise, according to The Verge. The case will be watched as a signal of how AI adoption affects headcount narratives in high-growth tech.

09 · TechCrunch

Khosla-backed robotics startup Genesis AI has gone full stack, demo shows

TechCrunch reports that Genesis AI, after raising a $105 million seed round, unveiled its GENE-26.5 robotics model and a demo using robotic hands. The announcement reflects the renewed push to bring foundation-model techniques into embodied AI.

10 · TechCrunch

Anthropic and OpenAI are both launching joint ventures for enterprise AI services

TechCrunch reports that both companies are partnering with asset managers to more aggressively market enterprise AI products. The enterprise AI market is moving from tools toward packaged service channels and distribution partnerships.
