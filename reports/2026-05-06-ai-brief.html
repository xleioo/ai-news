行业 AI 今日新闻

01 · Reuters

AMD forecast sparks AI-driven rally in US chipmaker stocks

AMD’s stronger outlook lifted U.S. semiconductor shares as investors read the guidance as evidence that demand for AI compute remains resilient. The story also points to AI-related data-center spending as a core market driver.

02 · Reuters

Big Tech to invest about $650 billion in AI in 2026, Bridgewater says

Bridgewater’s analysis estimates Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Microsoft will spend roughly $650 billion this year to scale AI infrastructure. The figure underlines how capital intensity has become the defining feature of the current AI cycle.

03 · The Verge

Meta is working on an OpenClaw-like AI agent for regular people

The Verge cites reporting that Meta is developing an internal agent project called “Hatch,” alongside an agentic shopping tool for Instagram. The direction suggests consumer-facing AI agents are becoming a platform priority rather than a side experiment.

04 · The Verge

AMD’s revenue jumps as data-center sales become the primary growth driver

AMD reported a sharp revenue increase, with Q1 data-center sales reaching $5.8 billion. The Verge notes AMD’s framing that data centers now anchor revenue and earnings growth, with AI agents adding demand for compute.

05 · The Verge

A 40,000-acre data center project was approved in Utah

A hyperscale data-center plan in Box Elder County moved forward despite local opposition. The project illustrates how AI infrastructure buildout is colliding with power, land-use and community concerns.

06 · The Verge

Google’s AI architect lived rent-free in Elon Musk’s head

The Verge’s trial coverage highlights DeepMind’s Demis Hassabis as a repeated point of concern in Musk/OpenAI testimony. The piece connects today’s AI competition to the industry’s earlier governance and talent conflicts.

07 · The Verge

Microsoft and OpenAI’s famed AGI agreement is dead

The Verge reports that Microsoft and OpenAI have dropped a long-running AGI clause while renegotiating their relationship. The change matters because contract structure is now as strategic as model capability in frontier AI.

08 · The Verge

2026 AI investments will cost more than the Moon landing

The Verge frames planned AI infrastructure spending by Meta, Microsoft, Amazon and Alphabet as historically large when compared with landmark U.S. capital efforts. The story reinforces how quickly AI economics have shifted from software margins to infrastructure scale.

09 · TechCrunch

Meta buys robotics startup to bolster humanoid AI ambitions

TechCrunch reports Meta acquired Assured Robot Intelligence to strengthen its work on AI models for robots. The move points to a broader push from language and vision systems into physical AI.

10 · TechCrunch

Elon Musk’s AI expert witness in the OpenAI trial fears an AGI arms race

TechCrunch covers testimony dynamics around Musk’s OpenAI case, including concerns from his AI expert witness about competitive AGI escalation. The legal fight continues to double as a public record of how top AI labs think about risk and control.
