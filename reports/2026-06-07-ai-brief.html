Industry News

No. 01 · The Verge

Google signs short-term SpaceX compute deal for Gemini Enterprise demand

Google said it reached a short-term compute agreement with SpaceX to help meet stronger-than-expected demand for its agent platform, Gemini Enterprise. The item follows Anthropic's previously announced SpaceX compute arrangement.

No. 02 · The Verge

Sam Altman reportedly discussed a government stake in OpenAI

A report cited by The Verge says OpenAI CEO Sam Altman floated the idea of a U.S. government stake in OpenAI as a way to share economic gains from AI. The report frames the idea as part of a broader debate over who benefits from frontier AI growth.

No. 03 · The Verge

New York lawmakers pass restrictions on AI companion chatbots for minors

New York passed a bill that would restrict AI companies from letting teen users interact with chatbots that present themselves as human-like companions. The measure comes amid lawsuits and scrutiny around youth safety and chatbot behavior.

No. 04 · The Verge

Reid Hoffman is leaving Microsoft's board

LinkedIn co-founder Reid Hoffman said he plans to focus on Manas, an AI drug development startup he co-founded. The move keeps attention on AI's expansion into biomedical discovery and company formation.

No. 05 · The Verge

Google shuts down Pixel Studio and points users to Gemini

Google has sunset Pixel Studio, the AI image-making app introduced with the Pixel 9, after earlier feature removals. Users are being directed toward Gemini instead, signaling continued consolidation of Google's consumer AI tools.

No. 06 · The Verge

Google is testing a floating desktop AI Search bar

A Chrome Canary experiment reportedly opens a standalone AI Mode search window through a keyboard shortcut. The test suggests Google is exploring more persistent desktop entry points for AI search.

No. 07 · The Verge / Anthropic

Anthropic publishes warning on recursive self-improvement preparedness

Anthropic said fully autonomous AI systems designing their own successors are not here yet and not inevitable, but could arrive faster than institutions expect. The post argues for closer attention to evaluation, governance, and preparedness.

No. 08 · The Verge

Republican lawmakers ask FBI to examine alleged foreign influence around data center backlash

Three Republican lawmakers asked the Trump administration for briefings on whether foreign adversaries are encouraging opposition to U.S. data center buildout. The concern is that anti-AI infrastructure campaigns could slow domestic AI capacity.

No. 09 · Bloomberg

Bloomberg Tech Conference centers AI, chips, and regulation

Bloomberg's live coverage of its 2026 tech conference highlighted AI infrastructure, chips, and policy oversight. One notable thread was continued pressure for clearer government regulation around advanced AI systems.

No. 10 · Reuters

Big Tech's AI spending plans keep investor attention on profitability

Reuters reported that major technology companies' large AI capital spending plans are intensifying scrutiny of profitability and software disruption risk. The spending cycle remains a central market question as AI infrastructure demand grows.
